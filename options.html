Site access

FocusGeek shows its own calm page on blocked sites it has access to, and asks for access one site at a time when you add them. Sites without access are still blocked, but the browser shows its own “blocked” page there, and tabs already open on them are left alone until you reload them.

Private windows

Browsers keep extensions switched off in private windows unless you allow them. To block sites there too, open FocusGeek’s details on your browser’s extensions page and turn on “Allow in Incognito” (Chrome, Brave), “Allow in InPrivate” (Edge) or “Run in Private Windows” (Firefox).

FocusGeek has no account and sends nothing anywhere. Your lists, schedule and counts stay in your browser.
